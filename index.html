FAQ

Good questions.

What do I need to send you?

Just an idea. A topic, a rough script or a few references is enough. If you already have a finished script, send that and we will build around it.

How is the price worked out?

Your first video can be our Starter package: one video up to 60 seconds for $75. Longer videos are $75 per minute, sound design and music included. You get a fixed price before we start, so there are no surprises later.

What is the difference between your channel and your service?

Inside the Systems is our own Instagram channel, where we publish factual history stories and creative visuals. Separately, we make videos for clients as a production service. When you order a video, it is made for your own channel or brand.

Can my brand sponsor your channel?

Yes. Sponsorships on our own Instagram are quoted individually, based on reach, audience and deliverables. Send us a message and tell us about your brand.

How long does a video take?

It depends on the length and style. We confirm a delivery date with you before we begin.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Two rounds of revisions are included in every video.

What formats do you deliver?

Vertical 9:16 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, or wide 16:9 for YouTube and websites. Tell us where it will be posted.

Can I publish it anywhere?

Yes. You get full rights to publish the finished video on any platform.
